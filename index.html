Create Your Character
Three steps to join the campaign
1
Download Your Sheet

This is your personal character sheet — a blank template. Open it in any browser to edit all your stats, inventory, and notes.

2
Build Your Character with AI

Copy this prompt and send it to your AI (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.). It will interview you one question at a time, verify your stats, and end with a full summary you can use to fill in your sheet.

You are helping me build my D&D 5e character sheet. Interview me — ask one question at a time to gather everything I need. After each answer, confirm what you've recorded, then ask the next. If any stats seem off (e.g. a modifier that doesn't match the score), flag it and help me fix it. Work through these in order: 1. Character name, race, class & level, background, alignment 2. All 6 ability scores — verify each modifier is correct 3. Combat stats: AC, Initiative, Speed, Max HP, Hit Dice, Passive Perception, Gold (GP), Carrying Capacity 4. Saving throw and skill proficiencies 5. Weapons — name, attack bonus, damage dice, notes 6. Class features, subclass features, racial abilities — with full descriptions 7. Equipment and any magic or special items 8. Personality Traits, Ideals, Bonds, Flaws 9. Backstory — ask follow-up questions to flesh it out When done, print a clean summary, one field per line: Character Name: ... Race: ... (every field filled in) Start by greeting me and asking for my character's name.
3
Fill In & Hand Off to the DM

Open your downloaded sheet in a browser — all fields are editable, just click and type. Fill everything in using your AI conversation. When done, send the file to the DM and they'll add you to the party.
