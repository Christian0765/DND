Use AI
Download a template, have an AI fill it, then upload it here
  1. 1
    Download the character sheet template
    ⬇ Download example-sheet.html
  2. 2
    Send it to an AI assistant
    Open the downloaded file in a text editor, copy all the contents, and paste it into any AI (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.) with a prompt like:
    "I want to create a D&D character. Before filling out the sheet, please ask me lots of questions to build out their lore — their name, race, class, backstory, personality, motivations, secrets, relationships, fears, and history. Ask follow-up questions based on my answers to really flesh them out. Once we've explored the character fully, fill in the attached HTML template using everything we discussed. Fill all the f-* fields and update the inventory JSON at the bottom with starting equipment that fits their story." [Paste the full contents of example-sheet.html after this message]
  3. 3
    Save the AI's response and upload it here
    Copy the AI's completed HTML, save it as a .html file, then drop it below — fields and inventory will be imported automatically.
